AI-IZ

Practical AI skills for study and campus life.

DateFriday, 2 October 2026
VenueBilik Mesyuarat, Kolej Ibu Zain

Presented by OrynLab, this interactive workshop helps students write better AI prompts, connect AI to useful tools, and use it more efficiently and responsibly. Short explanations, live demonstrations and group activities make the concepts accessible to beginners.

01

Prompting techniques

Students learn to give AI a clear persona, task, context and output format. We cover using examples, organizing pasted information, asking clarifying questions and improving an initial answer through follow-up prompts.

In practice: A 10-minute Prompt Lab compares “Plan my week” with a more specific request. Students review the before-and-after answers and discuss what improved.
02

AI + MCP: connecting tools

We introduce Model Context Protocol (MCP) as a way for AI assistants to work with external tools. Students see how connectors give access to apps, while skills provide reusable instructions for completing tasks.

In practice: Build a brief for a KIZ event, use a connected AI assistant to create a Canva poster, and watch a facilitator demonstrate a pitch deck built with an AI coding tool and a slide skill.

03

Token-saving techniques

Students explore tokens—the chunks of text AI processes—and how long prompts, chat history and images affect usage. We teach practical habits: request a suitable answer length, include relevant context, group related questions and start a fresh chat when changing topics.

In practice: Use a live token counter with English, Bahasa Melayu, emoji and code. Compare a lengthy prompt with a concise version that keeps the same task and essential details.
04

AI Challenge: team quiz

A 10-question team quiz revisits prompting, connected tools and token usage. Timed questions, answer explanations and a scoreboard help reinforce the learning in an engaging format.

Responsible use throughout: Check AI-generated facts, protect personal information, follow lecturers’ rules on AI, and review permissions before allowing connected tools to act.

By the end, students should be able to:

Write clearer promptsDescribe a task with useful context and a clear output format.

Explain connected AIUnderstand how an assistant can work with apps such as Canva.

Reduce unnecessary usageKeep prompts focused while retaining important information.

Evaluate AI responsesCheck accuracy and use AI with privacy and academic integrity in mind.

Preparation for the session

Participants: Bring a charged phone or laptop, internet access, an AI chat account and a Canva account. No coding experience is needed; the coding demonstration is facilitator-led.

Check account access and the Canva connection before the session; keep the prepared demonstration ready.
